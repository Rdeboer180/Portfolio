# Portfolio voice audit

Audited August 30, 2026.

## The read

Reading this as positioning and case-study copy for hiring managers and design leaders, anchored on the systems and products Ryan actually built, prioritizing specificity, authorship, evidence, and restraint.

The clearest version of Ryan's voice already existed in the Notes. It names the artifact early, admits the cost, lets an odd detail stay odd, and keeps the claim close to something a reader can inspect. The weakest copy did the opposite: it named a capability, assembled a polished set of three benefits, and described the work at the altitude of a job description.

## Skills used

- `ryan-taste`: artifact first, evidence beside the claim, clear ownership, admitted cost, and systems that survive implementation.
- `mentor-taste`: hiring signal, visible proof, end-to-end ownership, and restraint around AI claims.
- `humanizer`: corporate abstraction, forced groups of three, inflated conclusions, negative parallelism, uniform rhythm, and generated-sounding transitions.

The installed `ryan-taste` package is incomplete. Its top-level `SKILL.md` is present, but the voice guidelines, notes corpus, output targets, and preflight references named by that file are missing. For this pass, the published Notes served as the sentence-level voice corpus.

## Representative rewrites

### Process

Before:

> I ask the right questions before a single pixel moves. I map business goals to user research, surface the real problem early, and align stakeholders across product, engineering, and brand so everyone's building the same thing.

After:

> Before I open Figma, I need to know what is actually stuck. I compare the business goal with user evidence, name the constraints, and get product, engineering, and brand working from the same problem.

### Internal tooling

Before:

> Agentic internal application development: a connected layer of plugins and web applications that streamlined repetitive design work, standardized project communication, and reduced manual review cycles.

After:

> I built three internal tools that removed repeat setup and review work: a Figma metadata plugin, a production-accurate crop simulator, and a componentized presentation system.

### About

Before:

> I built my foundation intentionally.

After:

> Design had my attention before it became my job: high school electives in visual communication, an internship at a local broadcast station, and eventually graphic design and web animation at Kendall.

## Rules applied across the pass

- Name the artifact before the capability.
- Replace "I am good at" language with a decision, action, or shipped result.
- Keep metric scope and attribution caveats intact.
- Say what Claude or ChatGPT accelerated, then say what Ryan chose, checked, rejected, or owned.
- Do not turn every paragraph into a thesis, a list of three, and a polished conclusion.
- Preserve technical detail when it proves the claim; cut it when it only inventories the stack.
- Leave attributed recommendations in the speaker's own words.
- Preserve open status, private boundaries, and missing metrics instead of writing around them.

## Remaining human decisions

1. The site still repeats five closely related process surfaces: homepage process, About story, About process cards, proficiency dock, and Notes. The copy now uses one voice, but the content architecture still repeats the same argument. A future design pass could remove one or two surfaces rather than editing them again.
2. The unified `ryan-taste` skill should be reinstalled or completed with its missing reference files. The site itself currently contains a better voice corpus than the installed skill package.
3. The longest PlayDraft approach sections are dense by choice and backed by specific evidence. They no longer read generic, but a hiring manager may still skim them. That is a narrative-density decision, not an AI-writing problem.

## Verification boundary

This audit judges how the copy reads. It does not claim to detect who originally wrote a sentence. "AI-sounding" here means the copy relies on repeatable language patterns that hide Ryan's specific judgment, not proof that a model authored it.
