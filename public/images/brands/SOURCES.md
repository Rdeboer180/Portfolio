# Project brand assets

Added October 6, 2026. Artwork is copied unchanged; display sizing lives in
`src/styles/components/_project-brand.scss`.

- **Tire Rack:** `tire-rack.png` from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tire_Rack_logo.png), which attributes the asset to Tire Rack's official website. The official site blocked direct asset requests. Shown at about 67px wide on cards and 148px in company introductions; original is 188 × 39. Employer-logo display follows the existing unlock state.
- **Heatherwood:** `heatherwood.svg` downloaded from [the official website's header asset](https://www.heatherwoodacademy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/HeatherWood.svg). Transport gzip was decoded; SVG artwork is unchanged.
- **PlayDraft:** `playdraft-on-white.svg` copied from Ryan's `Claude/PlayDraft/implementation/category-round-system/playDraftLogos/SVG/logo_onWht.svg`. Original white-background mark, paired with the name in portfolio type.
- **LoopStack:** `loopstack.svg` copied from this repository's `public/loopstack-demo/loopstack-mark.svg`, paired with the product name.
- **Bolus Binder:** `bolus-binder.png` copied from Ryan's `Claude/BolusBinder/assets/brand/bolus-binder-primary-app-icon.png`, the app icon referenced by that project's `app.json`. This is the product's own artwork, not the separate T1D Hub clinic mark.
- **WheelRack:** plain portfolio type, as requested; no dealer logo.
- **Overscroll Tactics:** `overscroll-tactics.svg` copied unchanged from [the official site's mark](https://overscrolltactics.com/assets/img/wordmark.svg), paired with the studio name.

Cards place compact brands in the existing CTA row to avoid another content row.
Card marks use 80% of the initial compact scale, with a further optical reduction
for Tire Rack's heavy uppercase wordmark. The PlayDraft card has no separate
App Store download row.
Company introductions use a larger mark followed by a thin horizontal divider.
