What this page claims

  • Three materials, one light. The sleeve's specular, the foil ring and the face's light path all answer the same pointer. Nothing is baked into the artwork; the two screenshots are untouched files from the study.
  • The card leaves its own slot. It slides up behind the plastic first, so the part still in the mouth stays glossed and the part above it goes crisp. Only once it is clear does it come forward, scale and turn.
  • Foil lives at the edges. A pale prismatic ring and one moving light path. The face stays silver and the type stays dark, which is what makes the reference read as a card and not a hologram.
  • The back is worth the turn. Overview, role, stack, status, three outcomes from the study's takeaways, a real recap capture and the only link. No thumbnail repeated.
  • Every word is from the study. Title, subtitle, the three rows, the year and the stream label are the card data and the metrics array for playdraft. No HP, no scores, no month.

Where it still differs from the reference

  1. Pastel foil, not saturated rainbow. The reference's border is brighter. I kept it pale so the artwork holds at slot size. Owner call.
  2. No HP or attack numbers. The reference's right-hand values are invented. The rows carry the study's real values instead, so the right column is uneven (Launched, 4, 2). Astra to judge whether that rhythm is acceptable or the rows need a different shape.
  3. Date footer is the year only. The study records 2026, not a month.
  4. Artwork is two panes. The turn state and the Candy shield, rather than the reference's football montage. This is the hero track's recommendation, not an approved composition.
  5. Mobile keeps the desktop proportions. At phone width the card is wider than it is on the desktop page, so nothing is cut. The reference's shorter mobile crop was not needed; Opus should confirm in the real stack.
  6. Lock, filters, routing and the password challenge are absent. Those are Opus's half of the sequence, on top of this proof.
  7. Edge-on is a 3px painted edge. Convincing at speed, thin in a paused still. A thicker edge needs a real side face per edge; worth it only if Astra wants the still.
