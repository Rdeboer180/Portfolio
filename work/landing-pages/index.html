AEM Landing Page System & SEO Template Framework

Make landing pages a system, not a fire drill.

Designed 50+ landing pages personally, then built the governed AEM template system that two junior designers now use. Turnaround moved from ~1 month to 1–2 weeks for complex pages and 1–2 days for simple launches.

Every landing page started from scratch. No templates, no patterns, no scalable process.

  • the client uses landing pages to answer high-intent searches for tire sizes, categories, and narrow product needs.
  • SEO and partner teams needed those pages quickly, but every request still started from an empty Figma file and a thin brief.

Outcome

I designed and built more than 50 pages before turning the recurring decisions into templates. Two junior designers use them now. Complex pages take one or two weeks instead of about a month, standard pages about a week, and simple launches a day or two. SEO reporting tied one new 40-inch tire page to more than $10,000 in annual revenue. There was no earlier page to compare against, so I treat that as evidence of new reach, not a clean design-attribution claim.

  • A brief is often a tire type, a keyword list, and one product photo. The templates are built to make that enough.
  • When the template cannot do what a page needs, that becomes a project request, not an exception.
Time to Live~1 month → 1–2 weeks for complex pages, 1 week for standard, 1–2 days for simple launches.
